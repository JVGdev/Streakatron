# Vision

## The problem

The author, a computer science student, struggles to stay consistent with both studies and personal projects. Every classic failure mode applies:

1. Forgetting, or never starting.
2. Overwhelm: not knowing what to do next.
3. Missing a day, then dropping the habit entirely.
4. Taking on too many projects at once.
5. Motivation fading once the novelty wears off.

Most productivity tools fail in one of two ways: they cost more effort to maintain than the work they track, or they punish a single missed day hard enough that quitting the app becomes the rational move.

## The idea

One question, answered every time the app opens: **"What's the smallest meaningful thing I can do right now — and does it count?"**

- **Project management makes showing up go somewhere real:** areas → projects → goals → tasks, with Kanban, list, calendar, schedules and countdowns, and `@` mentions that link anything to anything.
- **Gamification rewards showing up:** XP for real effort, forgiving streaks, quests of several types sized to your own days.
- **A cosmetic reward layer keeps it fun long-term:** every quest grants a loot box; collectibles furnish a personal bedroom; a wardrobe shows off medals and prizes.
- **Friends keep you accountable** (later): friend streaks that grow only when both of you show up.

## Who it's for

- **Now:** the author, as the only user, on desktop (and phone over Tailscale).
- **Later:** potentially a product for students and self-directed learners/builders. Decisions are judged against that future (multi-user, server-authoritative rewards, mobile path), while scope stays personal-scale until the daily loop is proven.

## Principles

1. **Lower friction than the work it tracks.** Seconds to capture or start; keyboard-first.
2. **Forgiving, never punishing.** Missing a day never makes quitting rational. XP is never taken away.
3. **Reward effort, not busywork.** No farming by splitting, bulk-closing or re-completing tasks.
4. **Focus over abundance.** Today shows a few items; only a few projects can be active.
5. **Light core loop, cosmetic meta-layer.** No RPG stats or combat; rewards are cosmetic.
6. **Rewards follow real effort, and are never sold.** No paid loot, ever.
7. **Quests reward the work you actually do** — sized from your own days, never piling up work faster than it gets done.
8. **Product-ready from day one, personal-scale for now.**
9. **Dogfood early.** Use the MVP daily; let real use decide what comes next.

## Where this came from

- Brainstormed and refined in the first planning session (2026-11-03); see `progress.md`.
- Ideas from the user's `observations.txt`: quest types, loot boxes with rarities, the bedroom, friend streaks, the wardrobe.
- Lessons from the user's earlier app **Quire** (a worldbuilding/writing app with XP, quests and streaks), especially how its quests did and didn't fit real use (plan.md §11).
